      What do the symbols mean?
      ⟳
      Ticks itself from your save file.
      ✋
      Palworld doesn't record these — click a marker to tick it by hand.
      Reference
      Dungeons, treasure maps, warp points, skill fruit and bounties reset or respawn, so there's nothing to complete and no score. Look them up while you play; tick one if you want it greyed out.
      ●
      Sets what a category does with markers you've found: ● shown, ◐ dimmed, ○ hidden. Shift-click sets every category at once.
